Frieren season 1 was a beautiful and well executed story about the passing of time and the process of learning to appreciate life. So many endearing characters. The show is primarily a case of great execution in storytelling and pacing, taken to the maximum level (it dethroned fullmetal alchemist: brotherhood as the number 1 anime on [myanimelist](https://myanimelist.net/topanime.php) after 10 years).
