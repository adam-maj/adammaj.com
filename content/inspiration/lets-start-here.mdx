Lil Yachty, who was previously known for mumble rap and a few low intention-density hits, released this psychedelic rock album from out of left field. It is quite an auditory experience. Not much of lyrical quality but just incredible and creative sound design and great female singers that add a lot of texture. My favorite songs on it are the black seminole and we saw the sun.
