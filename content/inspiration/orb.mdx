About the triumph of the heliocentric theory over the geocentric theory, and how it corresponded with the one of the most significant shifts in belief and power in human history with the transition from Christianity to the Englightenment. Does not follow a single cast of characters but rather studies the development of an idea over time, where the pursuit of truth transcends generations. Reflection on the nature of science, the beauty of existence, the meaning of truth, the willingness to die for your beliefs, capitalism and modernity, and more.
