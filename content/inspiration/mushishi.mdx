An episodic show about a lonely medicine man who travels through the forest searching for primordial creatures (Mushi) that are causing troubles for people so he can cure them. Very peaceful and aesthetically unique. My favorite episode is _The Sea of Writings_, though it should only be watched after seeing at least 3 or 4 other episodes.
