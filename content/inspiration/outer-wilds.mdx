Outer Wilds is an open world exploration game where you play as an alien exploring your solar system to discovery the history of your civilization. It is best experienced directly and is very sensitive to spoilers.

Its now my favorite artwork across all media, and the reason I felt compelled to make this page. A multi-exceptional masterpiece, with visionary sound design, storytelling, and gameplay that represents true innovation in each dimension. The game has left me with a unforgettable imprint of its world, aesthetic, and message.

After playing, it is worth watching the documentary too which adds yet another layer of appreciation to the intentionality, creativity, and love that went into the game.
