_Dorohedoro_ means mud-sludge in Japanese, which gives a sense for the grimy aesthetic the anonymous mangaka Q Hayashida tried to create with this story. I find the world to be unusually unique and full of character. It taught me what a truly generative world looks like (a world with it's own simple but out of distribution rules that still create a wide variety of unique phenomena).
