A fresh modern hip-hop album from Jim Legxacy, a rising UK underground artist. Highly creative and novel music videos, and a legitimately new sound that fuses afro, r&b, UK rap, pop, etc. The album also has soul, as the artist is reflecting on the recent passing of his sister as well as his come up. I found the album randomly from a [reaction by Dontai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nOSLzZeoNcc). My favorite songs are 3x and sos.
