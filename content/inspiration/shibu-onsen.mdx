A charming bathhouse town in the Nagano prefecture in Japan with natural hot-spring water from the mountains and many Ryokan's where 1 pass gets you into all of them. Private room at the Sakeya Ryokan was the best experience of my life, and then finding the building that inspired the bathhouse in Spirited Away right next door made this an even more magical night.
