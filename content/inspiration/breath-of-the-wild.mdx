My first taste of a truly exploration based open world, where you can actually go anywhere and find interesting content. I loved the art style. I found the storytelling, enemy design, and boss design to be poor, but the art, sound design, and exploration made the game refreshing regardless. The greatest innovation of this game is the map design philosophy where key visual points of interest guide the player toward content.
